Headwater FAQ

Everything you want to know — flavor, function, and how Headwater supports daily performance.
Benefits
What is Headwater?
Headwater is a lightly sparkling beverage formulated with Lion’s Mane and Chaga mushroom extracts to support cognitive function and overall physiological resilience.
Why mushrooms?
Lion’s Mane and Chaga contain natural compounds studied for supporting brain communication, cellular protection, and immune balance.
How does it work?
Headwater delivers concentrated Lion’s Mane and Chaga extracts that support key biological systems involved in thinking, stress adaptation, and cellular health.
How soon will I notice benefits?
Many people notice a calm, clearer sense of focus early on. More significant benefits build gradually over one to three weeks of consistent use.
What will I feel?
Most people report calm focus, clear thinking, and steady mental energy without jitters or a crash.
Usage
Is this an energy drink?
No. Headwater contains no caffeine and no synthetic stimulants.
How is this different from coffee or energy drinks?
Coffee and energy drinks create short-term stimulation. Headwater supports steady focus and resilience.
How is this different from nootropic supplements or powders?
Most nootropics rely on isolated compounds or stimulants. Headwater uses whole-mushroom extracts for nutritional support.
When should I drink it?
Anytime you want clean mental energy — mornings, at work, before training, or during long days.
Do I need to cycle it?
No cycling is necessary. Headwater does not build tolerance.
Ingredients
Do you use real mushroom extracts?
Yes — certified organic fruiting-body extracts, not mycelium on grain.
Where is it made?
Produced in the United States with certified organic mushroom extracts.
Does it contain sugar or sweeteners?
No added sugar and no artificial sweeteners.
Is it carbonated?
Yes — lightly effervescent for refreshing daily drinking.
Performance
Is it suitable for professional athletes?
Yes. Headwater contains no banned stimulants and supports neural recovery and resilience.
Can this replace coffee?
Some people do replace coffee with Headwater, especially for smoother, jitter-free focus.
General Questions

Typically several hours, depending on the person.

Shelf-stable. Best served cold.
